What this screen is

This is the control room view. The other window — the street — is the digital twin of what is happening on the road. This one watches it, warns you before something needs a decision, and tells you what each option is likely to cost.

  1. Open index.html in a second window. That is the street.
  2. Press Two ambulances below, or do it from the street.
  3. This screen raises an alert with a countdown and your options.
  4. Pick one. It is applied on the street, and the outcome is logged.

If the two windows cannot talk, this screen says so at the top right and every control still works — it just cannot see the street.
