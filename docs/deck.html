Where it stops

Five documented gaps

    City-wide sensors and cameras cost a fortunethe barrier to adopting adaptive control at all
  • 02
    One blocked camera degrades the junction until it is repaired
  • 03
    Switching timing plans — and talking to legacy controllers — wastes time
  • 04
    Rare events are exactly what a learned model has never seenaccidents, closures, manual diversions
  • 05
    Mixed traffic breaks queue estimationtwo-wheelers and autos, no lane discipline
