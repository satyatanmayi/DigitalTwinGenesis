Where they stop

PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING

Five documented limits

  • 01
    Sensors and cameras at every junction cost a fortunewhich is why most Indian cities do not have them
  • 02
    A failed or blocked detector degrades that junction until it is repaired
  • 03
    Switching timing plans — and talking to legacy controllers — wastes time itself
  • 04
    Rare events are handed back to a human on a radioaccidents, closures, waterlogging, manual diversions — the bad day
  • 05
    Mixed traffic breaks queue countingtwo-wheelers and autos, no lane discipline

What is next, and what it is honestly for

  • NEXT
    Many priority requests at once · green-wave coordination · a real junction from OpenStreetMap · SUMO cross-validation
  • NN
    A neural network that learns ranking, not safetytrained inside this simulator, because you cannot train a controller on a real city. It does not drive the signals and it does not make the simulation accurate — calibration does that.
  • QUBO
    Phase choice under conflict constraints maps to a QUBOthe form quantum annealers take, with published traffic work behind it. Quantum-ready formulation, solved classically today.
