    A neural network that learns ranking, not safetytrained inside this simulator, because you cannot train a controller on a real city. It does not drive the signals and it does not make the simulation accurate — calibration does that.

    Phase choice under conflict constraints maps to a QUBOthe form quantum annealers take, with published traffic work behind it. Quantum-ready formulation, solved classically today.

Backup · where quantum fits

BACKUP

Quantum-ready, solved classically

  • WHERE
    Choosing phases for many junctions at once, not one at a timedeciding each junction independently is what produces local optima
  • FORM
    One binary variable per junction per phase, conflict constraints, delay objectivethat is a QUBO — exactly the form a quantum annealer accepts
  • WHY
    The search space grows exponentially with junctions and horizonfour junctions: a classical solver wins. Fifty junctions with a real horizon: cities fall back to heuristics
  • STATUS
    Published QUBO formulations for signal control exist, with D-Wave runs on 100–500 vehicle instances. Nothing deployedwe would write the QUBO and solve it with simulated annealing. The same formulation submits to a quantum annealer unchanged

If asked whether it will be faster: unproven at our size, and we will not claim it. The formulation outlives the hardware question.
